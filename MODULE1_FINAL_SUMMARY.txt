DATABASE TABLE STRUCTURE
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

TABLE: supplier_identification

COLUMNS:
  id (INT)                          - Primary Key, Auto-increment
  supplier_name (VARCHAR 150)       - UNIQUE supplier name [REQUIRED]
  contact_email (VARCHAR 150)       - Email address [REQUIRED]
  certifications (VARCHAR 500)      - Comma-separated certs [optional]
  risk_level (ENUM)                 - Low/Medium/High [default: Medium]
  phone (VARCHAR 20)                - Phone number [optional]
  notes (TEXT)                      - Additional notes [optional]
  status (ENUM)                     - Active/Inactive/Archived [default: Active]
  created_at (DATETIME)             - Auto-timestamp
  updated_at (DATETIME)             - Auto-timestamp

INDEXES:
  idx_risk_level, idx_status, idx_supplier_name, idx_created_at
