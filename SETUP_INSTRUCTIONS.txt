DATABASE SCHEMA:
================================================================================

Table Name: supplier_identification

Columns:
- id (INT, Primary Key, Auto Increment)
- supplier_name (VARCHAR 150, UNIQUE) - Supplier company name
- contact_email (VARCHAR 150) - Contact email address
- certifications (VARCHAR 500) - Comma-separated certifications
- risk_level (ENUM: Low, Medium, High) - Supplier risk assessment
- phone (VARCHAR 20) - Contact phone number
- notes (TEXT) - Additional notes
- status (ENUM: Active, Inactive, Archived) - Supplier status
- created_at (DATETIME) - Record creation timestamp
- updated_at (DATETIME) - Last update timestamp

Indexes:
- idx_risk_level, idx_status, idx_supplier_name, idx_created_at
